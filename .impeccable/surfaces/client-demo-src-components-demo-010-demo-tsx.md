# Voice workspace

Mode: Operate

## Direction contract

**Thesis.** Make voice models recognizable by their artwork and place the controls for the selected voice beside the library.

**Own-world.** Use soft white surfaces, charcoal type, a pale lilac workspace accent, pill controls, and artwork from installed models.

**Story.** Pick a voice, check the selected model and audio devices, then start conversion and read its live signal metrics.

**First viewport.** A slim utility header and centered product title lead into the model library and live session. Selecting a library card updates the larger portrait and its controls. On narrow screens the sections stack.

**Form.** seed: none (pinned). “Fastwork.com’s portfolio look” for “the main workspace and its model and settings dialogs.”
